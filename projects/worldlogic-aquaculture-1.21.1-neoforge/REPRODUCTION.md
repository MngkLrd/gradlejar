# Fish reproduction design, informed by Let Fish Love 1.1.1
Reference examined: uploaded NeoForge 1.21.1 JAR, MIT metadata, FishBreedGoal, FishLayRoeGoal, RoeBlock, FishBreedingCap, EntityEvents, fish_food tags and roe_hatch_data JSON. No third-party code or textures copied; implementation and tuning are independent.
Adopted now: per-species reloadable food tags, server-owned feeding and timed satiety with consumption/cooldown; species registry has maturity, nursery, incubation and reproduction interval parameters. Maturity is currently a predicate used by regression, not live spawning.
Not adopted: arbitrary feeding immediately induces love, instant adult hatch, persistent unbounded fish, vanilla-water-only placement, generic uniform egg durations, non-realistic feed (e.g. beetroot for cod), any shared mutable parent state on block singleton.
Future phases: reserve offspring against population capacity before creating a clutch; persist clutch identity, species, parent IDs, habitat, age, viability and individual water kind; progress incubation only while suitable; pause or lose viability on dry, pollution, unsuitable salinity/temperature; hatch to juvenile cohort first, not automatic adults; transport roe via item data without resetting age or cloning; ensure an unhatched clutch cannot occupy both inventory and world; native water must not be overwritten. Per-species nursery category, season, food and maturity gates. No explicit external mod compatibility until the ownership boundary and ledger integration are tested.
Balance values are gameplay priors, not biological field estimates. In particular kelp/seagrass are abstract feeds for planktonic groups until plankton and live invertebrate food exist. The earlier uniform vanilla-cod graphics are still incomplete.
