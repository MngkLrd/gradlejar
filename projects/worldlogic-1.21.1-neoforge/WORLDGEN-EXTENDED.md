New biome classes partition the same climate fields. Compare exact base height at identical coordinates and seeds against the immutable RC22 preset; intentional local cave mouths/soil features are separately reviewed. Do not remove old registry IDs needed by existing saves. This rule overrides older plans to amplify mountain height. One selectable Realistic preset for new World Logic worlds, with legacy IDs retained for old saves. Full generated-world and client checks remain required before release-quality claims.

## Altitude and meteorology

Block coordinates are not atmospheric metres. Proposed compressed atmospheric scale 10 m per block above the actual generator sea level, applied consistently to pressure, environmental lapse rate, cloud base and altitude-dependent effects. Keep physical block distances, movement and structural physics unchanged. Standard-reference pressure and temperature are not actual local weather; use the existing regional baseline and local air temperature to avoid double-applying elevation cooling. Provide additive altitude API and explicitly document units. The new scale requires calibration and addon integration; do not silently change the RC21 public method semantics. Below ground, ventilation, groundwater and geothermal conditions use depth below local surface, not altitude alone; do not imply hypoxia from depth or Y alone. Cloud rendering target Y440 for first high preset, later derive from meteorology; allow mountain intersection in low cloud/fog. Minecraft 1.21.1 needs a client-side dimension effects/render hook rather than newer dimension cloud_height field. Preserve normal cloud height in other worlds. Test cloud render in actual client, not server only.

## Geology-led caves

Use surface/depth and drainage fields, lithology and fracture permeability to control networks; geology in Core, vegetation in Terrarian. Start with sedimentary limestone/dolomite and relatively impermeable strata, then karst passages and fracture-controlled vertical shafts. Distinguish vadose channels above water table and saturated phreatic passages below; derive hydraulic gradients from water table and catchments, no blanket flooding below sea level. Cave density should be a depth-relative bounded mask over connected corridor networks, not independent random spherical chambers. Add occasional collapse/sinkholes, seasonal springs, dry relic passages, rare lava tubes only in volcanic provinces later. Deep rock should contain fewer large voids except specific geological contexts. Preserve accessible surface entrances, ores, structures, cave biomes and vanilla aquifers until replacement has equivalent evidence. Groundwater and geology precede full dynamic dissolution; no per-block simulation of centuries of erosion.

References: British Geological Survey, https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mendips/caveskarst/caveform.htm and https://www.bgs.ac.uk/groundwater/water-resources/groundwater-resources-in-the-uk/karst-aquifers/ . Version constraints: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Noise_settings and https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dimension_type .

## RC23 research implementation
New versioned preset `worldlogic:earthlike_extended`, matching custom dimension/noise bounds -256..511. Preserve lowlands up to Y110 and amplify higher relief 1.6x. Geological deposit geometries follow USGS deposit model concepts: https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/b1693/html/bull1nzi.htm and https://www.usgs.gov/publications/porphyry-copper-deposits-world-database-map-and-grade-tonnage-models . Coal follows BGS sedimentary basin model https://www.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/minerals/mine-and-quarry/coal/ ; karst follows BGS https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mendips/caveskarst/caveform.htm . Distances/grades are game-scale provisional parameters, not measured global frequencies. Existing preset IDs unchanged. Draft only until chunk-generation integration and QA.

Integrated initial geological carving and ore placement: 5 province families, local crossing passages, carbonate chambers, rare shafts, static water horizons, lithology proxies using vanilla blocks and eight ore body geometries. Existing standard ore features (all targets vanilla ores) and noise veins replaced only in the extended preset. Chunk-confined writes. Limitations: fixed district boundaries, analytic finite passage shapes, coarse regional water table (not dynamic groundwater), no large interdistrict hydraulic connectivity, no placer sediment model, no real ore-grade/tonnage calibration, vanilla cave biomes/structures retained, no client cloud changes yet. Registry/sampler and actual synthetic chunk block-write assertions added; real newly generated worlds, ore economy, compatibility and render checks remain release-quality gaps.

Client effects registration raises only extended-world clouds to Y440; old dimension effects unaffected. The extended biome temperature density uses the same additional-elevation correction as the public climate API. Sedimentary layers use sandstone rather than volcanic tuff. Client visual QA remains NOT_RUN.

Deposit size and richness now use bounded lognormal variability, inspired by USGS grade/tonnage model distributions (https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/b1693/html/bull1nzi.htm), with explicitly game-specific medians and limits. Added actual synthetic chunk block-write assertions for all eight ore families, preserving cave air and bedrock. Full real-world ore frequency and gameplay calibration remain pending.

## RC24 unified Realistic
New preset `worldlogic:realistic_unified`, one selectable World Logic preset; legacy registry entries remain loadable. 51 own Overworld biome classes using multi-noise intervals, based on climate, moisture, continentalness, erosion and depth. RC22 density above Y=-40 retained (including near-surface noise cave openings); RC23 geological carving and ore placement enabled, deep base density extended. Mountain amplification removed for this preset. New biome surface rules map legacy parents to custom IDs. No vanilla plant feature placement: Terrarian owns flora. Minimal animal spawn tables and structure tags are provisional; full structure/ecology compatibility and generated-world checks pending. Classification inspiration: Olson et al., Terrestrial Ecoregions of the World (2001), https://www.sfu.ca/geog315-new/readings/Olson.pdf . Game classes are not a literal recreation of the 867 ecoregions.
