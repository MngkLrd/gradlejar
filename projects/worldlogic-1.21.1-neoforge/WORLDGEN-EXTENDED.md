# Earthlike extended vertical world — next preset design, 2026-11-27

Status: proposed engineering baseline, not implemented. Existing preset IDs, dimension type, noise settings and generator equations must stay unchanged. Current earthlike_climate declares sea level 63, min_y -64, height 384 and uses vanilla cave density functions, carvers and aquifers. A reported water surface at 75 must be diagnosed against actual generator sea level and local water level, not assumed to be the ocean.

## New preset

Create a separate versioned preset and dimension type; keep the Overworld dimension key, Nether and End unchanged. Initial target min_y=-256, height=768, max build Y=511, sea level=63; matching noise settings and dimension type bounds. Aim for lowland Y65–110, uplands Y110–220, exceptional summits Y250–380. Do not merely extend the noise settings: old top slide starts at Y240, old vertical gradient spans -64..320, vanilla ore/carver/aquifer and surface rules have height assumptions. Review each and maintain compatibility with ordinary biome features. Do not overwrite global minecraft registries to alter other presets. New world only; never silently migrate old saves or mix generation profiles at chunk boundaries.

## Altitude and meteorology

Block coordinates are not atmospheric metres. Proposed compressed atmospheric scale 10 m per block above the actual generator sea level, applied consistently to pressure, environmental lapse rate, cloud base and altitude-dependent effects. Keep physical block distances, movement and structural physics unchanged. Standard-reference pressure and temperature are not actual local weather; use the existing regional baseline and local air temperature to avoid double-applying elevation cooling. Provide additive altitude API and explicitly document units. The new scale requires calibration and addon integration; do not silently change the RC21 public method semantics. Below ground, ventilation, groundwater and geothermal conditions use depth below local surface, not altitude alone; do not imply hypoxia from depth or Y alone. Cloud rendering target Y440 for first high preset, later derive from meteorology; allow mountain intersection in low cloud/fog. Minecraft 1.21.1 needs a client-side dimension effects/render hook rather than newer dimension cloud_height field. Preserve normal cloud height in other worlds. Test cloud render in actual client, not server only.

## Geology-led caves

Use surface/depth and drainage fields, lithology and fracture permeability to control networks; geology in Core, vegetation in Terrarian. Start with sedimentary limestone/dolomite and relatively impermeable strata, then karst passages and fracture-controlled vertical shafts. Distinguish vadose channels above water table and saturated phreatic passages below; derive hydraulic gradients from water table and catchments, no blanket flooding below sea level. Cave density should be a depth-relative bounded mask over connected corridor networks, not independent random spherical chambers. Add occasional collapse/sinkholes, seasonal springs, dry relic passages, rare lava tubes only in volcanic provinces later. Deep rock should contain fewer large voids except specific geological contexts. Preserve accessible surface entrances, ores, structures, cave biomes and vanilla aquifers until replacement has equivalent evidence. Groundwater and geology precede full dynamic dissolution; no per-block simulation of centuries of erosion.

References: British Geological Survey, https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mendips/caveskarst/caveform.htm and https://www.bgs.ac.uk/groundwater/water-resources/groundwater-resources-in-the-uk/karst-aquifers/ . Version constraints: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Noise_settings and https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dimension_type .

## Acceptance gates

Separate world-generation revision; test real newly generated worlds on at least two seeds (not only sampler fixtures), coastline/waterline, altitude distribution, bedrock and ceiling, seam continuity, cave cross sections/connectivity, dry/wet controls, ore distribution and structure spawn. Measure chunk generation time, RAM, TPS and client rendering; wider vertical bounds increase potential section count. Check addon API, save/reload and existing old preset unaffected. No full release until tests pass. No automatic porting to other versions/loaders.
