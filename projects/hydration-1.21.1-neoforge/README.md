# WL: Hydration 0.3.0 development candidate
Empty main/off hand: right click water to drink 250 mL. Vessel in main hand: aim at a source and right click to fill in one action; otherwise hold right click to drink 250 mL. Sneak-right click with another vessel in offhand to transfer main to offhand up to capacity. Sneak-right click with charcoal filter offhand treats up to 3 L, consuming one filter. Sneak-right click lit campfire with heatproof vessel to boil; offhand copper condenser selects distillation. Stay within four blocks and keep vessel unchanged in main hand; interrupted treatment produces no output. Boiling evaporates 5%, preserves modeled salt, distillation yields 70% with removed brine abstracted as waste. Composite contamination risk, not separate real pathogens and sediment.
