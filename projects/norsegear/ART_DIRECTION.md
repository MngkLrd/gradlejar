# Norse Gear: Vanilla Clarity visual specification

- **24 native 16x16 PNG sprites:** every pixel is authored on the final grid; alpha is 0 or 255; textures are not downscaled screenshots or generated promotional images.
- **Steel:** cool shadow #56646B, base #89999C, light #B8C5C6, edge #F1F3E7.
- **Timber:** deep #754526, base #A96937, golden edge #D8974F.
- **Leather:** burnt brown #60331F, body #9A542B, edge #D17B42.
- **Shields:** two paint hues, steel rim and raised boss; distinguish Celtic / round / bossed from the GUI silhouettes.
- **Sculpting rules:** structural 2-5 px clusters, one-pixel edge highlight only where useful, no antialias, no random speckles or semi-transparent shading.
- **Equipped armor:** 64x32 UV atlas separate from item icons, 3-tone metal, distinct warm leather, chainmail alpha cutouts, open nasal helmet.
- **3D models:** maintain their game cuboids and real-time rotations; use matching warm wood and cool steel palettes, not dark synthetic gray materials.
