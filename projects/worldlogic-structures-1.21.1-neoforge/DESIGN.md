# Architecture pilot design and review
27 deterministic design variants in eight regional families; four reusable construction grammars (dwelling, tower, collapsed footprint, raised platform), not 27 independent handcrafted templates. All are abandoned, small, preindustrial and without storage containers or loot. The catalogue in StructuresApi.java is the editable size/roof/family list. Native registered family IDs allow locate. Site policy: bounded local height and shallow-water screening, at most two designs and four rotations, supported plinth/stilt columns, entrance toward dry ground, no terrain flattening. Platform variants require actual shallow water; caves and open sea excluded in this iteration.
Climate reference principles: BME Vernacular Dwellings https://habitatio.epitesz.bme.hu/en/iii-koznepi-lakohelyek/ (log walls in cold forest, courtyard types in dry climates); National Museum of Finland Seurasaari https://suomenkansallismuseo.fi/en/museums/seurasaari-open-air-museum/ (traditional log buildings); global comparative study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11058517/ (climate relationships with wall, floor and roof form). These are conceptual references, not copied plans or distributed assets. Individual elevations/interiors and Minecraft reference/client visual inspection remain pending.
Cold: compact spruce/timber, slate plinth, steep gable, small window, hearth/flue; boreal: framed log houses, timber tower, collapsed barn. Temperate: plaster/timber, limestone base, brick pitched roofs, masonry tower. Arid: sandstone, packed mud, small windows, flat roof or low lean-to, courtyard remains. Tropical: raised jungle timber, eaves, masonry ruin. Wetland: mangrove wood and anchored piers, raised hut or broken boardwalk; alpine: gneiss plinth/walls, compact timber roof; coast: light timber on limestone, signal tower and broken shallow-water platform.
No custom textures or new block IDs. Core RockContent.java confirmed gneiss_bricks, slate_bricks and limestone_bricks. Vanilla block and shape fallbacks are coherent. Normal windows open or broken intentionally; doorway clear; tower ladder to upper floor. Localized roof/wall damage, rubble and cobwebs, unfueled furnace and craft bench. No borrowed NBT or art.
Review required before expanding: render representative house, tower, ruin and raised platform in each suitable climate; inspect all sides, entry clearance, support, roof and final block state, and check new-chunk generation across actual Core terrain and saved reload. Automated catalogue checks are NOT graphical or natural-world certification.

Follow-up source review: explicit eave beam, supported ruin rubble, full approach footprint and shared per-candidate height cache, temperature-sensitive coastal and wetland timber. Two-seed structure-start and negative climate tests are authored but not executed. No visual inspection claimed.
