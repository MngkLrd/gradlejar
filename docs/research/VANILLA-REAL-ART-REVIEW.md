| Case | Preferred side | Specific difference and remaining concern |

| R7-008 organic topsoil | B | A has parallel diagonal chocolate-colored ridges, reading like a patterned surface. B uses irregular aggregates with broader warm/dark transitions and a small muted inclusion, closer to dirt/rooted dirt. Some large motifs still recur in tiling; inspect in-world. |
| R7-009 loam | A | B repeats diagonal bright/dark stripes. A contains varied clay-brown aggregate edges and subdued gray detail. More organic at a distance; recurring isolated gray inclusion still merits in-world inspection. |

All five nonpreferred sides need rework for the observed mechanical repetition in this intended ground-material context. Preferred sides are relative improvements, not unconditional passes. At native distance and 3x3, natural aggregates generally vary orientation, widths, lengths and local values. Do not prohibit directional motifs for other materials (e.g. bark, planks, sediment layers where appropriate). Preserve material intent: peat darker organic mass, frozen soil embedded cold patches, silt fine subdued particles, humus rich organic clods, loam coherent warm soil. No minimum color count, no random noise added to break repetition.

## Hydration item snapshots — specific corrections

| Case | Intended object | Static findings and next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R7-001 A | Clay jug | Recognizable stepped neck, right-side handle and rounded body. Warm highlights spread across the belly and outline reads clearly. Compare clay matte treatment to copper canteen: soften the brightest patch, clarify rim/neck thickness and use a subdued terracotta plane rather than metallic-looking broad shine. |
| R7-002 A | Wooden cup | Clear dark opening, thick light rim and handle; wood reads through golden and brown shades. A few nearly checkerlike marks on the body risk generic surface noise. Relate short grain cues to the cup construction and rim, not arbitrary dots. Closest to usable at sprite scale, still compare in inventory. |
| R7-005 A | Copper canteen | Recognizable neck and rounded body. The large warm highlight and lower-right teal patch help distinguish metal/oxidation, but the silhouette and light pattern are close to clay jug. Make metal highlights more localized, define shoulder/rim or seam, integrate patina along a plausible edge; do not merely add more green. |
| R7-006 A | Charcoal filter | Dark upper cavity and muted container are legible, but may read as an open jar with contents rather than a filter. Clarify a perforated layer or functional closure only if supported by actual design; dark charcoal grains should read as media, not a featureless hole. |
| R7-010 A | Leather waterskin | Tapered neck and asymmetrical soft pouch silhouette are useful. Broad orange light patch and heavy lower edge can read as clay/copper instead of leather. Follow the pouch contour with a restrained fold and a short seam/closure cue, avoid generic metallic shine. |
| R7-011 A | Copper condenser | Two open pipe ends and bent outline distinguish a conduit. The bright inner corners and orange bend suggest copper, but the square opening and broad highlight make it look like a soft orange tube. Define an inner bore, darker turning plane and narrow material highlights; keep patina near an edge, not arbitrary dots. |

For the six items, these are actionable observations rather than final ACCEPT/REJECT labels: intended 3D geometry, function and actual game render were unavailable. At the family level, compare all icons together, especially clay jug/copper canteen/leather waterskin; their different material response and construction must remain visible at 1x, not only after zoom. Do not make all objects high-contrast or require every outline to contain a seam. The source branch owners may use these findings without changing the research specimens.

## Completion boundary

The research has established real examples, a corpus, a reproducible comparative protocol and concrete feedback. It has not demonstrated independent reviewer accuracy or a successful new redraw validated by owner/independent reviewer. A definitive evaluation needs a fresh review context with unseen cases, hidden key, separate exact owner-approved/rejected labels and recorded confidence. No tool in this session launches such a separate reviewer; do not manufacture a reviewer identity or score. Existing Art Review Lock remains evidence validation, not aesthetic certification. Do not make owner manual reviews mandatory for routine mod development. Do not introduce any new build gate here.
